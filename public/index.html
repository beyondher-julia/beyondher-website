For girls ages 12-18

Every girl has a beyond.

Beyond Her is a community of girls ages 12-18 who lift each other up, make handmade cards for girls who are going through things, and earn volunteer hours along the way. We inspire girls to go beyond the limits others set for them and the doubts they carry.

Our mission

Empower girls through mentorship, education, leadership, and access to opportunities.

Beyond Her started in 2026 when Julia saw that girls today were feeling alone or lost. Today we connect girls with mentors, skills, and a community that expects more of them than the world sometimes does.

A note from the founder

Hi, I'm Julia

I started Beyond Her because I believe no girl should have to navigate her future feeling alone, uncertain, or without someone in her corner. I wanted to create something that reminds girls of their potential and shows them that their dreams are worth pursuing.

As a student myself, I understand how important it is to have encouragement, guidance, and people who believe in you. I wanted to turn that belief into something bigger: a community where girls can find mentors, discover new opportunities, develop their voices, and feel supported as they grow.

Every girl who joins Beyond Her deserves to feel seen, supported, and inspired to chase her dreams. I hope you find a community here that reminds you just how much you're capable of. 💗

Julia, Founder of Beyond Her
